How it will work

  1. One path. It is written by the same function as every cohort card, private.post_week2_recognition, through a new 'cohort_complete' branch, so test accounts never post and errors are logged.
  2. Once, after Week 6. It posts when all six weeks are complete, straight after that member's Week 6 card, with its own one-time key <slug>:cohort_complete:<cohort_id>. It can never post twice.
  3. The card no longer names the coach. The notification still says who recognised her ("Rebecca Louise recognised you in the community").
  4. Notifications and tabs. 'cohort_complete' is added to notify_community_recognition and cohort_wins_launch_guard, so it lands in the right cohort tab.
  5. The app ships the new card before the database change goes live. An older app shows it as a plain shout-out, which is safe.

Save & share: what the build needs

  1. Where: app/distributor/[slug].tsx, inside the existing "Cohort" section, only when cohortComplete(member.cohortProgress) is true. That is the same check that shows "Completed" today.
  2. The image: the app draws the no-confetti card at 1080 × 1350 off-screen and saves it as a picture, then opens the phone's share sheet. Save Image puts it in Photos, and Instagram, Facebook and Messages appear as normal.
  3. Heads-up: the app doesn't have the two small add-ons this needs yet (react-native-view-shot to make the picture, expo-sharing for the share sheet). Adding them needs a new App Store build. It can't go out as an over-the-air update.
  4. Members could get the same button on their own card later, if you want it.
